#HACKChristmasOutputs & evaluation

In 24 hours, four teams built something to help Nonoy, 19, from Jaro, find the true meaning of Christmas. Here's what they made and presented on Saturday, Nov 21,, followed by participants' key takeaways and photos from the weekend. Jump to the takeaways ↓

The outputs

Four teams, four ways to reach Nonoy

Each team picked a format, built it in 24 hours, and pitched it on Saturday afternoon.

Prototypes

A picture of what's possible

Everything here was made in just 24 hours, so these are first drafts, not finished products. They show where each idea could go. Teams are encouraged to keep building on them: test with real people like Nonoy, refine the content, and launch.

From the event evaluation

Key takeawaysfrom participants

What stayed with participants, drawn from the evaluation they filled in right after the closing on Saturday.

  1. Creativity can carry the gospel. Reels, posts, songs and games are real ways to share Jesus, and God-given talents are meant for it.
  2. Christmas is an open door. People are more willing to listen this season, so it's the time to share.
  3. Make it approachable. The good news should be easy for someone who has never heard it, like Nonoy, to come close to.
  4. Better together. Meeting other Christian creatives and building with them was part of the blessing.
  5. Learning by doing. Mentoring plus a real challenge turned ideas into content in 24 hours.
